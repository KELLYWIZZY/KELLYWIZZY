### 📊 Featured Projects

#### 1. SUPER STORE ANALYSIS PROJECT
Analyzed Superstore sales data (2011 - 2014) to find what drives sales, profit and losses.
- Cleaned raw Superstore data with Power Query
- Built a full Power BI dashboard with KPIs and slicers
- **Key Results:** Total Sales: **12.64M** | Total Profit: **1.47M** | Orders: **25,035** | Quantity Sold: **178K**
- **Key Insight:** Technology is the most profitable Category, Copiers is the top Sub-Category, and Consumer segment brings 51.48% of sales
- **Key Insight:** Sales grew every year from 2011 to 2014, but profit stayed low — discount is eating profit

[👉 View the project here](https://github.com/KELLYWIZZY/SUPER-STORE-ANALYSIS)
